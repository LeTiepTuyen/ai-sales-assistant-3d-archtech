# Client Brief - Alpha Factory

## Client Overview

Client name: Alpha Factory  
Industry: Electronics manufacturing  
Primary contact: Operations Manager  
Business context: Alpha Factory operates a mid-sized electronics assembly facility with multiple production lines and frequent coordination between operations, engineering, quality, and management teams.

## Current Situation

The client wants a clearer way to understand production-line status and communicate operational issues across teams. Managers currently rely on manual reports, screenshots, and verbal updates, which makes it difficult to quickly explain bottlenecks or compare improvement scenarios.

## Pain Points

- Production-line status is difficult to present clearly to senior management.
- Operations and engineering teams do not always share the same visual understanding of factory issues.
- Improvement discussions are often abstract because stakeholders cannot easily compare scenarios.
- Sales and management presentations need more professional visual material.
- The client is not ready for a large enterprise transformation project and prefers a focused pilot.

## Business Goals

- Improve production-line visibility for management discussions.
- Create a visual way to explain operational problems and improvement ideas.
- Support scenario-based conversations before committing to process changes.
- Build a professional pilot that can later expand if the business value is confirmed.

## Expected Proposal Direction

The client is interested in a Digital Twin and 3D visualization pilot for one representative production line or process area. The proposal should explain the solution in business-friendly language and recommend a phased approach.

## Items to Confirm

- Exact production-line scope for the first pilot.
- Available diagrams, layouts, process notes, or operational data sources.
- Stakeholders who will review the pilot.
- Required level of visual and simulation fidelity.
- Budget range and preferred delivery timeline.
- Success criteria for the pilot.
