# Client Brief - Orion Property Showcase

## Client Overview

Client name: Orion Property Showcase  
Industry: Real estate and property development  
Primary contact: Sales and Marketing Manager  
Business context: The client sells commercial property spaces and wants a stronger digital presentation experience for prospective tenants and investors.

## Current Situation

The sales team currently uses static renderings, floor plans, and slide decks. Prospective tenants often ask how spaces can be configured for different business uses, but the sales team cannot easily demonstrate those options during meetings.

## Pain Points

- Static materials do not fully communicate the property experience.
- Prospective tenants need help imagining different layout options.
- Sales meetings require more interactive and memorable presentation material.
- The client wants to differentiate the property from competing developments.

## Business Goals

- Improve client understanding of property layout and potential use cases.
- Support interactive sales conversations with better visual storytelling.
- Create a reusable digital showroom experience.
- Help the sales team present options more clearly and professionally.

## Expected Proposal Direction

The client is interested in 3D visualization and an interactive property showcase. The proposal should focus on sales enablement, visual communication, and phased implementation.

## Items to Confirm

- Property spaces and floor plans to include.
- Required visual fidelity and interaction level.
- Whether AR/VR is required or optional.
- Target audience for the showcase.
- Budget range and timeline.
