# Client Brief - Mekong Logistics Hub

## Client Overview

Client name: Mekong Logistics Hub  
Industry: Warehousing and logistics  
Primary contact: Business Development Director  
Business context: The client manages a regional warehouse and logistics operation that needs clearer visualization for facility planning, client presentations, and operational improvement discussions.

## Current Situation

The client often explains warehouse layout, traffic flow, and storage zones through static diagrams. These materials are difficult for non-technical stakeholders to understand during sales meetings and planning reviews.

## Pain Points

- Static diagrams are not persuasive enough for client-facing presentations.
- Stakeholders need a clearer view of space usage, traffic movement, and operational zones.
- Management wants to compare layout options before investing in physical changes.
- Sales teams need reusable visual material for prospective warehouse clients.

## Business Goals

- Present warehouse operations in a more professional and visual way.
- Help stakeholders understand layout decisions and operational flow.
- Support scenario discussions for facility improvement.
- Create reusable sales demonstration material.

## Expected Proposal Direction

The client is interested in 3D visualization and a possible Digital Twin-style experience for warehouse layout communication. The proposal should start with a focused visualization pilot and avoid unsupported claims about live system integrations until data availability is confirmed.

## Items to Confirm

- Warehouse area and zones to visualize.
- Available floor plans, process notes, and operational constraints.
- Whether live or near-real-time data is required.
- Main audience for the demo: internal management, external clients, or both.
- Timeline and budget range.
