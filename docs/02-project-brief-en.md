# Project Brief — AI Sales Assistant for 3D Archtech

## Project Goal

Build a moderate-scope demo AI Sales Assistant that helps sales users retrieve information from internal company materials, explain technical services in business-friendly language, and generate proposal drafts based on previous proposal templates and client inputs.

## Target Users

- Sales team members
- Business development staff
- Demo evaluators / university business course audience

## Main Demo Scenarios

1. **Knowledge-base Q&A:** Sales asks about 3D Archtech services, technical terms, portfolios, and case studies.
2. **Proposal generation:** Sales enters client information and receives a structured proposal draft based on old proposals and company materials.

## MVP Expectations

- Professional chatbox UI
- Proposal generator form and preview
- Admin data source page
- PDF/XLSX ingestion from local files
- RAG-based retrieval, not model fine-tuning
- Gemini-based generation if feasible
- DOCX and PDF/print-friendly export
- Local-first development and public demo deployment plan

## Non-Goals

- No enterprise-grade CRM integration
- No multi-tenant user management
- No paid database/vector infrastructure unless approved
- No fine-tuned model unless clearly justified
